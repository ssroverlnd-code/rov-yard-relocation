# Communication Plan — ROV Yard Relocation

## 1. Objectives

- Keep every team lead informed of schedule changes within 24 hours.
- Ensure all vendors receive confirmed bookings at least 48 hours in advance.
- Provide weekly progress updates to all stakeholders.
- Maintain a single source of truth for decisions and changes.

---

## 2. Channels

| Channel | Purpose | Participants |
|---------|---------|--------------|

## 5. Vendor Communication Protocol

| Event | Communication | Timing |
|-------|---------------|--------|
| Booking request | Email with load details, date/time, contact person | ≥ 72 hours before |
| Booking confirmation | Email confirmation with PO number | Within 24 hours |
| Day-before reminder | WhatsApp/phone call | Day before move |
| Day-of arrival | Phone call to vendor + Logistics Coordinator | At dispatch |
| Completion | Email with signed manifest | Same day as move |
| Issue / delay | Immediate phone call + follow-up email | Within 30 minutes |

## 9. Communication Rules

1. **One source of truth** — all schedule changes communicated via WhatsApp group and confirmed in weekly report.
2. **No silent changes** — any deviation from the plan must be flagged to PMO Lead immediately.
3. **Written confirmation** — all vendor bookings, authority applications, and landlord agreements must be confirmed in writing (email or signed document).
4. **Escalation within 24 hours** — unresolved issues escalate to PMO Lead within 24 hours, then to Project Sponsor within 48 hours.
5. **Document everything** — decisions, changes, incidents, and agreements recorded in project folder.
