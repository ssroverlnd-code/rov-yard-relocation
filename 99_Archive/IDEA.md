# IDEA — ROV Yard Relocation

Workplan and update on moving the SSRover ROV yard from Kota Masai (Pasir Gudang) to Masjid Tanah, together with inventory updates.

## Purpose

Relocate the ROV yard out of Pasir Gudang after repeated burglaries (hand tools, metal stock and copper cabling stolen) to a new, purpose-laid-out yard at Masjid Tanah, Melaka — with a complete inventory, classified storage and zero further loss.

## Why

- The Pasir Gudang yard has suffered **repeated burglaries** — tools, metal stock and copper cabling taken.
- Inventory is **disorganised** across three storage zones with no systematic segregation.
- The new yard is an opportunity to fix both: a secure layout and a disciplined inventory.

## What success looks like

1. Zero theft or loss during the move.
2. 100% of items inventoried, classified and tagged before anything moves.
3. New yard laid out in five zones with a locked secure cage for high-value items.
4. Full authority compliance at both sites.
5. Old yard cleared and handed back within 8 weeks.
