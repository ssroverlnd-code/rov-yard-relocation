## 4. KEY ASSUMPTIONS & RISK NOTES

1. **Site accessibility:** Destination is assumed road-accessible. If ferry/barge (C4) is required, add RM 1,200.
2. **Move duration:** Estimated at 3 working days. Weather or permit delays may extend by 1–2 days (covered by E2).
3. **Asset condition:** ROV system assumed serviceable. If major faults emerge post-strip-down, E3 (RM 600) is the first call; beyond that, a separate variation order will be raised.
4. **Venue readiness:** Destination site must have power, water, and test-tank/pool access confirmed before move date. Delays due to site unrecoverable are excluded.
5. **Training schedule:** Move window planned to avoid disrupting P098/GMI training calendar; any rescheduling impact will be flagged to management.
6. **Currency & validity:** All estimates in RM; vendor quotations to be obtained before work commencement. Figures valid for 30 days from report date.
