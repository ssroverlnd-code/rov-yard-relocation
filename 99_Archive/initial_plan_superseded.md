# ROV Yard Relocation Project Management Plan

**Company:** SS Rover Sdn. Bhd.  
**From:** Pasir Gudang, Johor  
**To:** Masjid Tanah, Melaka  
**Planning period:** 19 October–14 December 2026 (8 weeks maximum)  
**Project lead:** Appointed Relocation Project Manager  
**Status:** Initial plan, subject to management approval, site readiness and vendor availability.

## 1. Project objectives and scope

Relocate engineering equipment, inventory, tools, selected office assets and documentation from the existing ROV yard to Masjid Tanah through a controlled, phased process. Address disorganized inventory and recent theft of tools, metals and copper cables.

Objectives:
1. Establish a verified inventory, including identification, condition, quantity, value and destination.
2. Secure high-value assets and prevent further losses.
3. Move heavy and oversized equipment safely using approved lifting and transport methods.
4. Sell, recycle or discard unwanted office furniture, scrap and obsolete items through authorized procedures.
5. Establish a secure, functional new yard with designated storage zones.
6. Complete reconciliation, clearance and handover within eight weeks or earlier.

**Scope boundary:** Regulatory and training approval implications of moving an IMCA-approved ROV training establishment must be checked separately before commencing affected activities at the new site.

## 4. Detailed relocation stages

### Stage 1 — Mobilization and immediate security (W1, 19–25 October)

**Lead:** PM and Yard Supervisor. **Support:** Management, Security, HSE, Inventory.

| Action | Owner | Deliverable |
|---|---|---|
| Appoint PM and functional leads | Management | Approved project organization |
| Kickoff and site walkthrough | PM / Yard Supervisor | Scope and action register |
| Secure vulnerable storage, locks, access, lighting and CCTV | Security / Facilities | Security inspection and temporary controls |
| Control all asset movements and review theft losses | PM / Inventory / Security | Authorization process and loss register |
| Establish stocktake template | Inventory | Controlled inventory sheets |
| Confirm new-yard representative and access | PM | Stakeholder contact register |

Move portable valuables to locked controlled storage pending shipment. Require authorization for all removals, including scrap.

**Gate 1:** Team appointed and temporary security implemented.

### Stage 2 — Inventory and classification (W1–W2, 19 October–1 November)

**Lead:** Inventory & Asset Control. **Support:** Technical, Finance, Yard Supervisor.

Stocktake warehouse, roofed yard and open yard by numbered section. Capture unique ID, description, quantity, location, condition, value/reference, dimensions/weight as applicable, category, packaging, destination and custodian.

| Category | Examples | Decision |
|---|---|---|
| A — High value | ROV electronics, sensors, copper cables, specialized tools | Secure, priority relocation |
| B — Heavy / oversized | ROV frames, machinery, skids | Engineered lifting and heavy transport |
| C — General equipment | Racks, workbenches, reusable workshop items | Standard transport |
| D — Loose items | Parts, fittings, fasteners, consumables | Count, label, bin and box |
| E — Documents / IT | Filing cabinets, computers, records | Controlled custody |
| F — Disposal / scrap | Furniture, obsolete/damaged materials | Authorized sale, recycle or discard |

Classify condition as **Serviceable**, **Repairable** or **Unserviceable**. Do not dispose solely on appearance; technical owner must confirm. Record discrepancies and potential burglary losses separately.

**Gate 2:** Inventory baseline and disposal candidates approved by Yard Supervisor, Finance and PM.

### Stage 3 — New-yard coordination, layout and readiness (W2–W4, 26 October–15 November)

**Lead:** PM / Facilities / Technical. **Support:** New Yard Owner, HSE, Security, Logistics.

| Action | Owner | Evidence |
|---|---|---|
| Confirm lease, access and permitted activities | Management / Yard Owner | Written agreement |
| Joint premises condition survey | PM / Yard Owner | Signed condition report |
| Measure gates, roads, spaces, load capacities | Technical / Logistics / Qualified Engineer | Verified dimensions and suitability |
| Prepare traffic, lifting and storage layout | Technical / HSE | Approved layout |
| Check fencing, locks, CCTV, lighting | Facilities / Security | Security functional |
| Confirm power, drainage, fire protection and emergency access | Facilities / HSE | Readiness checklist |
| Mark unloading and storage zones | Inventory / Facilities | Zones identified |

**Proposed functional zones:** A secure high-value store; B heavy-equipment area with suitable ground/floor capacity; C workshop/general equipment; D parts/consumables store; E locked records/IT area; F quarantine/inspection/disposal area; plus controlled entry, unloading and traffic circulation. Layout is conceptual pending site survey and engineering verification.

**Gate 3:** PM, Yard Owner and HSE approve receiving-site readiness before dispatch.

### Stage 4 — Vendor procurement, disposal and packing (W2–W4, 26 October–15 November)

**Lead:** Procurement / Logistics. **Support:** Finance, Technical, HSE, Admin.

| Work package | Action |
|---|---|
| Transport survey | Verify weights, dimensions, number and type of loads. |
| Vendor appointment | Compare technical capability, safety, insurance, cost and availability. |
| Heavy lifting | Approve equipment-specific lift plans, operators and rigging. |
| Packing | Procure pallets, bins, covers, labels, straps and protective materials. |
| Furniture | Approve and arrange sale or disposal without unnecessary relocation. |
| Scrap | Verify ownership, valuation and authorized disposal route. |
| Documentation | Prepare manifests, custody forms, disposal and shipment records. |

**Gate:** Vendor orders, disposal approvals, packing lists and shipment schedule ready.

### Stage 5 — High-value and sensitive assets (W4–W5, 9–22 November)

**Lead:** Technical / Inventory. **Support:** Security, Logistics, PM.

1. Segregate valuable assets; verify IDs and serial numbers.
2. Photograph condition; protect sensitive electronics from moisture, shock and ESD as applicable.
3. Prepare signed high-value manifest and dedicated secured transport.
4. Supervise dispatch with authorized release and two-person verification.
5. Verify receipt item by item, record discrepancies and lock assets in secure storage.

Copper cables and specialized tools must not be mixed with scrap or general loose loads.

**Gate 4:** All scheduled high-value assets accounted for, received and secured, or exceptions escalated.

### Stage 6 — Heavy and oversized equipment (W5–W6, 16–29 November)

**Lead:** Technical / Lifting Contractor. **Support:** HSE, Yard Supervisor, PM, New Yard Representative.

1. Confirm identity, weight, dimensions, lifting points and centre-of-gravity information.
2. Obtain technical approval for dismantling if required.
3. Approve equipment-specific lifting plan and risk assessment.
4. Verify crane/forklift capacity, rigging, personnel competence and ground conditions.
5. Confirm vehicle suitability, road access and permits/escorts if applicable.
6. Load, transport and unload under competent supervision and exclusion-zone controls.
7. Inspect and position equipment; document damage or missing components.

**Gate 5:** Major equipment delivered, inspected and safely positioned.

### Stage 7 — Loose items, office equipment and records (W6–W7, 23 November–6 December)

**Lead:** Inventory / Admin. **Support:** Technical, IT, Logistics, Finance.

| Item | Action |
|---|---|
| Hand tools | Verify and pack by category. |
| Fasteners and spare parts | Sort, count and place in labelled bins. |
| Consumables | Check condition, expiry and special handling. |
| Cables | Identify, inspect, protect and pack. |
| Filing cabinets | Review contents, secure records and transport approved cabinets. |
| IT | Back up data, disconnect, protect and relocate. |
| Furniture | Sell or dispose as authorized. |
| Unidentified items | Quarantine for technical/ownership review. |

Use sequential labels such as `SSR-MT-D-001`. Maintain document custody and update destination-bin records.

**Gate 6:** All retained items transferred and disposals documented.

### Stage 8 — Reconciliation, clearance and handover (W7–W8, 30 November–14 December)

**Lead:** PM. **Support:** All leads, Finance, HSE, both Yard Owners.

| Action | Evidence |
|---|---|
| Match dispatch and receipt | Final inventory reconciliation |
| Inspect engineering assets | Condition reports |
| Verify bins and storage locations | Updated location register |
| Test security and utilities | Operational readiness checklist |
| Resolve damage/loss claims | Claims and punch-list register |
| Inspect and clear old yard | Signed owner condition / clearance record |
| Close access and utilities | Handover confirmation |
| Submit project closure report | Sponsor-approved report and completion certificate |

**Gate 7:** New yard accepted, old yard handed over, project closed.

## 6. Documentation and movement control

Required registers/documents:
- Project charter and budget.
- Master inventory and condition register.
- Theft/loss and discrepancy register.
- New-yard layout and readiness checklist.
- Vendor contracts, insurance and transport documents.
- Lifting plans and risk assessments.
- Packing lists and shipment manifests.
- Movement authorizations and signed custody records.
- Disposal approvals and evidence.
- Compliance/permit register.
- Final reconciliation, clearance and handover report.

**Mandatory shipment sequence:** Inventory verified → packed and condition checked → movement authorized → dispatched → received and inspected → inventory location updated. A load is not complete until signed receipt against its manifest.

## 9. Acceleration strategy

Six to seven weeks may be achievable if teams inventory separate areas in parallel; site preparation and procurement overlap with inventory; approved scrap/furniture clears early; high-value assets move once secure receiving storage is ready; heavy loads are consolidated; and reconciliation occurs after each delivery. Do not bypass safety, compliance or site-readiness gates.

## 11. Final acceptance criteria

- All retained assets received at Masjid Tanah and their locations recorded.
- High-value items accounted for, with historic losses documented separately.
- Heavy equipment safely transported and positioned.
- Loose items and consumables organized and labelled.
- Unwanted furniture and scrap disposed of with approval.
- New-yard security, storage and applicable compliance requirements satisfied.
- Existing yard cleared and handed over in accordance with obligations.
- Management approves reconciliation, costs and project closure.

**Recommendation:** Treat this as a controlled engineering asset transfer and yard reorganization project, not a conventional office move. Prioritize asset accountability, safe handling and secure storage. Aim to finish major physical transfers by W7 and reserve W8 for reconciliation and formal handover.
