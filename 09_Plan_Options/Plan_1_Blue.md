# Plan 1 — Blue Team: Formal Engineering Asset Transfer

**Approach:** Gate-controlled, compliance-first, sponsor-approved engineering project.  
**Source:** `99_Archive/initial_plan_superseded.md` (initial plan — now archived)  
**Duration:** 8 weeks (19 Oct – 14 Dec 2026), with acceleration to 6–7 weeks possible  
**Status:** For team discussion and decision

---

## 1. Philosophy

Treat this as a **controlled engineering asset transfer and yard reorganization project**, not a conventional office move. Every asset is accounted for, every gate is approved, every movement is authorized in writing. Process discipline is prioritised over speed.

## 3. Inventory Classification (6 Categories)

| Class | Examples | Decision |
|-------|----------|----------|
| **A — High Value** | ROV electronics, sensors, copper cables, specialised tools | Secure, priority relocation |
| **B — Heavy / Oversized** | ROV frames, machinery, skids | Engineered lifting and heavy transport |
| **C — General Equipment** | Racks, workbenches, reusable workshop items | Standard transport |
| **D — Loose Items** | Parts, fittings, fasteners, consumables | Count, label, bin and box |
| **E — Documents / IT** | Filing cabinets, computers, records | Controlled custody |
| **F — Disposal / Scrap** | Furniture, obsolete/damaged materials | Authorized sale, recycle, or discard |

Condition codes: **SVC** (Serviceable) / **REP** (Repairable) / **UNS** (Unserviceable).  
**Rule:** Do not dispose solely on appearance — technical owner must confirm.

## 5. Stage Detail

### Stage 1 — Mobilization & Immediate Security (W1)

| Action | Owner | Deliverable |
|--------|-------|-------------|
| Appoint PM and functional leads | Management | Approved project organization |
| Kickoff and site walkthrough | PM / Yard Supervisor | Scope and action register |
| Secure vulnerable storage, locks, access, lighting, CCTV | Security / Facilities | Security inspection and temporary controls |
| Control all asset movements; review theft losses | PM / Inventory / Security | Authorization process and loss register |
| Establish stocktake template | Inventory | Controlled inventory sheets |
| Confirm new-yard representative and access | PM | Stakeholder contact register |

**Gate 1:** Team appointed and temporary security implemented.

### Stage 2 — Inventory & Classification (W1–W2)

- Stocktake warehouse, roofed yard, open yard by numbered section.
- Capture: unique ID, description, quantity, location, condition, value/reference, dimensions/weight, category, packaging, destination, custodian.
- Record discrepancies and burglary losses separately.
- **Gate 2:** Inventory baseline and disposal candidates approved by Yard Supervisor, Finance, PM.

### Stage 3 — New-Yard Coordination & Readiness (W2–W4)

| Action | Owner | Evidence |
|--------|-------|----------|
| Confirm lease, access, permitted activities | Management / Yard Owner | Written agreement |
| Joint premises condition survey | PM / Yard Owner | Signed condition report |
| Measure gates, roads, spaces, load capacities | Technical / Logistics / Engineer | Verified dimensions |
| Prepare traffic, lifting, storage layout | Technical / HSE | Approved layout |
| Check fencing, locks, CCTV, lighting | Facilities / Security | Security functional |
| Confirm power, drainage, fire protection, emergency access | Facilities / HSE | Readiness checklist |
| Mark unloading and storage zones | Inventory / Facilities | Zones identified |

**Gate 3:** PM, Yard Owner, HSE approve receiving-site readiness before dispatch.

### Stage 4 — Vendor Procurement, Disposal & Packing (W2–W4)

| Work Package | Action |
|-------------|--------|
| Transport survey | Verify weights, dimensions, number and type of loads |
| Vendor appointment | Compare capability, safety, insurance, cost, availability |
| Heavy lifting | Approve equipment-specific lift plans, operators, rigging |
| Packing | Procure pallets, bins, covers, labels, straps, protective materials |
| Furniture | Approve and arrange sale or disposal without unnecessary relocation |
| Scrap | Verify ownership, valuation, authorized disposal route |
| Documentation | Prepare manifests, custody forms, disposal and shipment records |

**Gate 4:** Vendor orders, disposal approvals, packing lists, shipment schedule ready.

### Stage 5 — High-Value & Sensitive Assets (W4–W5)

1. Segregate valuable assets; verify IDs and serial numbers.
2. Photograph condition; protect sensitive electronics (moisture, shock, ESD).
3. Prepare signed high-value manifest and dedicated secured transport.
4. Supervise dispatch with authorized release and two-person verification.
5. Verify receipt item by item; record discrepancies; lock in secure storage.

**Gate 5:** All high-value assets accounted for, received, secured, or exceptions escalated.

### Stage 6 — Heavy & Oversized Equipment (W5–W6)

1. Confirm identity, weight, dimensions, lifting points, centre of gravity.
2. Obtain technical approval for dismantling if required.
3. Approve equipment-specific lifting plan and risk assessment.
4. Verify crane/forklift capacity, rigging, personnel competence, ground conditions.
5. Confirm vehicle suitability, road access, permits/escorts if applicable.
6. Load, transport, unload under competent supervision and exclusion-zone controls.
7. Inspect and position equipment; document damage or missing components.

**Gate 6:** Major equipment delivered, inspected, safely positioned.

### Stage 7 — Loose Items, Office Equipment & Records (W6–W7)

| Item | Action |
|------|--------|
| Hand tools | Verify and pack by category |
| Fasteners and spare parts | Sort, count, place in labelled bins |
| Consumables | Check condition, expiry, special handling |
| Cables | Identify, inspect, protect, pack |
| Filing cabinets | Review contents, secure records, transport approved cabinets |
| IT | Back up data, disconnect, protect, relocate |
| Furniture | Sell or dispose as authorized |
| Unidentified items | Quarantine for technical/ownership review |

Sequential labels: `SSR-MT-[CLASS]-[SEQ]`. Maintain document custody and update destination-bin records.  
**Gate 7:** All retained items transferred and disposals documented.

### Stage 8 — Reconciliation, Clearance & Handover (W7–W8)

| Action | Evidence |
|--------|----------|
| Match dispatch and receipt | Final inventory reconciliation |
| Inspect engineering assets | Condition reports |
| Verify bins and storage locations | Updated location register |
| Test security and utilities | Operational readiness checklist |
| Resolve damage/loss claims | Claims and punch-list register |
| Inspect and clear old yard | Signed owner condition / clearance record |
| Close access and utilities | Handover confirmation |
| Submit project closure report | Sponsor-approved report and completion certificate |

**Gate 8:** New yard accepted, old yard handed over, project closed.

## 6. Authority & Compliance Screening

| Authority | Potential Issue | Lead |
|-----------|----------------|------|
| Majlis Perbandaran Alor Gajah (MPAG) | New premises jurisdiction, permitted use, licences, building/signage | Admin / Facilities |
| Majlis Perbandaran Pasir Gudang (MPPG) | Existing premises licence closure | Admin |
| DOSH / JKKP | Lifting machinery and workplace safety | HSE |
| JPJ | Vehicle and road transport requirements | Logistics |
| JKR / road authority | Oversize movement or access where applicable | Logistics |
| JBPM / BOMBA | Applicable premises fire safety | Facilities / HSE |
| DOE / JAS | Scheduled waste or regulated disposal, if any | HSE |
| **IMCA** | **Implications for any approved ROV training establishment or training location** | **Training Management** |

> ⚠️ **IMCA is flagged as a scope boundary:** Regulatory and training approval implications of moving an IMCA-approved ROV training establishment **must be checked separately before commencing affected activities at the new site.** See ADR-001.

## 7. Documentation & Movement Control

**Mandatory shipment sequence:**  
`Inventory verified → packed & condition checked → movement authorized → dispatched → received & inspected → inventory location updated`

A load is **not complete** until signed receipt against its manifest.

**Required registers:** Project charter, master inventory, theft/loss register, layout & readiness checklist, vendor contracts, lifting plans, packing lists, shipment manifests, movement authorizations, custody records, disposal approvals, compliance/permit register, final reconciliation report.

## 8. Risk Register

| Risk | Priority | Mitigation / Owner |
|------|----------|-------------------|
| Further theft | High | Immediate security, access logs, early valuable-asset move / Security |
| Missing or unidentified assets | High | Section stocktake and discrepancy register / Inventory |
| Lifting injury or equipment damage | High | Approved lift plans, competent contractors, exclusion zones / HSE |
| New yard not ready | High | Early survey and readiness gate / PM & Facilities |
| Transport delays or capacity | Medium–High | Verified load data, backup vendors / Logistics |
| Damage to ROV electronics | High | Technical packaging, condition records, insured transport / Technical |
| Wrongful disposal | Medium–High | Technical and Finance written approvals / Finance |
| Landlord or regulatory restriction | High | Early compliance and tenancy review / PM |
| Cost overrun | Medium | Quotation comparison, contingency approval and tracking / Finance |
| Slow reconciliation | Medium | Update records per delivery / Inventory |

## 10. Acceleration Strategy

Six to seven weeks may be achievable if:
- Teams inventory separate areas in parallel.
- Site preparation and procurement overlap with inventory.
- Approved scrap/furniture clears early.
- High-value assets move once secure receiving storage is ready.
- Heavy loads are consolidated.
- Reconciliation occurs after each delivery.

**Do not bypass safety, compliance, or site-readiness gates.**

## 12. Final Acceptance Criteria

- All retained assets received at Masjid Tanah and their locations recorded.
- High-value items accounted for, with historic losses documented separately.
- Heavy equipment safely transported and positioned.
- Loose items and consumables organized and labelled.
- Unwanted furniture and scrap disposed of with approval.
- New-yard security, storage, and applicable compliance requirements satisfied.
- Existing yard cleared and handed over in accordance with obligations.
- Management approves reconciliation, costs, and project closure.
