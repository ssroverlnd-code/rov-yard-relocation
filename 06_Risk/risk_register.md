## Incident Response Protocol

1. **Stop work** — secure the area
2. **Report** — notify PMO Lead within 15 minutes
3. **Document** — photos, written account, witnesses
4. **Assess** — determine if work can continue
5. **Escalate** — if injury, theft, or major damage → Project Sponsor immediately
6. **Insurance** — notify provider within 24 hours for any claimable incident
7. **Record** — log in risk register as incident
