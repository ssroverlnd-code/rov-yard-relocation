# ROV Yard Relocation — Master Moving Plan

> **Relationship to the adopted plan.** This document is the **operational execution baseline** — the Red team's 10-stage structure with per-stage checklists. **Plan 3 — Hybrid** (`09_Plan_Options/Plan_3_Hybrid.md`) is the **adopted plan**: it applies Blue-team governance (8 gates, dedicated HSE, IMCA/DOSH/DOE compliance) over this execution detail. Where the two differ on **stage numbering and gates**, follow Plan 3; where they differ on **execution detail** (packing, convoy, lifting), this document is the reference.

## 8-Week Relocation Schedule (Pasir Gudang → Masjid Tanah)

> **Rule of thumb:** Weeks 1–2 = Plan & Prepare · Weeks 2–3 = Approvals & Vendors · Weeks 3–7 = Execute Moves · Week 8 = Settle & Close

---

## Stage 1 — Foundation & Planning (Week 1)

**Objective:** Establish the project baseline. Define teams, open communication channels, kick off inventory, and draft the new yard layout concept.

| # | Task | Owner | Deliverable | Duration |
|---|------|-------|-------------|----------|
| 1.1 | Set up project folder, register, and comms channels (WhatsApp group, email thread) | PMO Lead | Project folder + communication group live | Day 1–2 |
| 1.2 | Walkthrough of ALL three storage zones + office with photos/video | Inventory Lead | Photo/video log of current state | Day 2–3 |
| 1.3 | Draft new yard layout concept (zones: heavy, valuable, consumables, skids) | Operations Lead | Sketch/layout draft | Day 3–5 |
| 1.4 | Register all authorities to be contacted and vendors to be shortlisted | PMO Lead | Authority contact list + vendor shortlist | Day 3–5 |
| 1.5 | Approve Stage 1 kick-off with all internal team leads | PMO Lead | Kick-off meeting minutes | Day 5 |

**Exit Gate:** Layout concept drafted · Inventory walkthrough complete · Authority/vendor lists opened

---

## Stage 2 — Inventory & Classification (Weeks 1–2, overlaps Stage 1)

**Objective:** Every item classified by value, size/weight, and security category. Items are **sorted first, then packed and tagged at package level** — the 5S sequence (Sort → Set in order). This is the single most important stage: nothing moves without a package tag and a contents list.

| # | Task | Owner | Deliverable |
|---|------|-------|-------------|
| 2.1 | Physical inventory of all items; sort wanted from unwanted, then pack and tag at package level (5S: Sort → Set in order) | Inventory Lead + team | Full inventory list (Excel/Sheets) + tagged packages |
| 2.2 | Classify each item: **A** = High Value / **B** = Heavy / **C** = Consumable / **D** = Office/Furniture | Inventory Lead | Classification column in inventory |
| 2.3 | Photograph and condition-note every Class A (valuable) package — sealed, with the tag visible | Inventory Lead | Condition report with photos |
| 2.4 | Verify quantities of copper cables, hand tools, metal stock against last known records | Operations Lead | Variance report |
| 2.5 | Flag items for salvage/disposal (broken, obsolete, unserviceable) | Operations Lead | Salvage list for approval |

**Exit Gate:** 100% of items inventoried and classified · 100% of packages tagged with contents lists · Class A packages sealed and photo-documented

---

## Stage 3 — Stakeholder Setup & Compliance (Weeks 2–3)

**Objective:** Secure all approvals, contracts, and insurance. Engage all external parties in parallel.

### 3A — Internal Teams

| Team | Leader | Responsibility |
|------|--------|----------------|
| **Lifting & Rigging** | Rigging Supervisor | Crane hire, safe lifting of Class B items |
| **Transport & Logistics** | Logistics Coordinator | Vehicle bookings, route planning, manifests |
| **Security & Valuables** | Security Lead | Class A item handling, escort, secure transport |
| **IT & Equipment** | IT Lead | IT equipment disconnect, packing, reinstall |
| **Warehouse & Storage** | Warehouse Lead | Packing, labelling, staging for dispatch |

### 3B — Logistics Vendors

| Vendor Type | Engagement |
|-------------|------------|
| Heavy haulage / low-loader contractor | Quote for Class B items (ROV components, structural) |
| General moving company | Quote for warehouse items, office equipment |
| Crane & rigging services | On-site lift at Pasir Gudang + offload at Masjid Tanah |
| Security escort firm | Armed/security escort for Class A convoy |

### 3C — Yard Owner (New Location — Masjid Tanah)

| Action | Owner |
|--------|-------|
| Confirm lease/agreement terms with landlord | PMO Lead |
| Confirm power, water, fencing, and lighting readiness | Operations Lead |
| Agree on yard handover date for layout work to begin | Operations Lead |
| Confirm any landlord restrictions (e.g., operating hours, heavy vehicle access) | PMO Lead |

### 3D — Authorities

| Authority | Purpose | Owner |
|-----------|---------|-------|
| **Pasir Gudang local council (MPPG)** | Move-out notification, trade licence surrender | PMO Lead |
| **Melaka/Masjid Tanah local council (MPMT)** | New yard trade licence / occupancy permit | PMO Lead |
| **Road transport authority (JPJ)** | Oversized load permits (if any Class B exceeds limits) | Logistics Coordinator |
| **Insurance company** | Goods-in-transit coverage + storage coverage at new yard | PMO Lead |
| **Fire & safety authority** | Storage compliance (esp. consumables, chemicals) | Warehouse Lead |
| **Environmental authority** | Disposal of unserviceable items (scrap metal, e-waste) | Operations Lead |

**Exit Gate:** All vendor contracts signed · All authority applications submitted · New yard access confirmed

---

## Stage 4 — New Yard Preparation (Weeks 3–4, overlaps Stage 3)

**Objective:** Get the new Masjid Tanah yard ready to receive items — layout marked out, security measures in place, storage zones defined.

| # | Task | Owner | Deliverable |
|---|------|-------|-------------|
| 4.1 | Install/upgrade perimeter fencing and lighting at new yard | Operations Lead | Fencing complete |
| 4.2 | Set up secure storage cage or container for Class A items | Security Lead | Secure cage installed |
| 4.3 | Mark out floor zones per approved layout (paint/tape lines) | Warehouse Lead | Zone markings complete |
| 4.4 | Install shelving and racking for consumables and hand tools | Warehouse Lead | Racking installed |
| 4.5 | Confirm power supply, water, and welfare facilities | Operations Lead | Utilities confirmed |
| 4.6 | Test CCTV or security monitoring | Security Lead | CCTV operational |

**Exit Gate:** New yard physically ready to receive classified deliveries

---

## Stage 5 — Sale & Disposal of Office Furniture (Weeks 3–4)

**Objective:** Clear all office furniture and IT equipment from the old yard. This happens in parallel with new yard prep.

| # | Task | Owner |
|---|------|-------|
| 5.1 | Sort office furniture: sell vs. discard | Operations Lead |
| 5.2 | Advertise sale of saleable items (online, auction, direct) | PMO Lead |
| 5.3 | Arrange scrap metal / e-waste collector for discards | Operations Lead |
| 5.4 | Disconnect and pack all IT equipment | IT Lead |
| 5.5 | Remove filing cabinet contents — sort: keep, archive, shred | Warehouse Lead |
| 5.6 | Confirm old yard office area cleared | PMO Lead |

**Exit Gate:** Office furniture sold or disposed · IT equipment packed and ready · Filing contents sorted

---

## Stage 6 — Valuable Items Move (Week 4)

**Objective:** Move all Class A (high-value) items first — copper cables, hand tools, precision instruments, ROV critical spares.

| # | Task | Owner |
|---|------|-------|
| 6.1 | Pack Class A items in sealed, tamper-evident crates with item-level manifest | Security Lead |
| 6.2 | Load into secure transport with security escort | Security Lead |
| 6.3 | Direct transit Pasir Gudang → Masjid Tanah (no intermediate stops) | Logistics Coordinator |
| 6.4 | Offload directly into secure cage at new yard | Security Lead |
| 6.5 | Reconcile manifest against inventory — sign-off | Security Lead + Inventory Lead |

**Exit Gate:** 100% of Class A items accounted for at new yard secure storage

---

## Stage 7 — Big & Heavy Items Move (Weeks 4–6)

**Objective:** Move all Class B (heavy) items — ROV components, structural steel, skids, large containers.

| # | Task | Owner |
|---|------|-------|
| 7.1 | Final crane booking and lift plan for each heavy item | Rigging Supervisor |
| 7.2 | Prepare each heavy item for lift (slings, frames, disconnect) | Rigging Supervisor |
| 7.3 | Load onto low-loader / heavy haulage with route plan | Logistics Coordinator |
| 7.4 | Obtain JPJ oversized load permit if required | Logistics Coordinator |
| 7.5 | Transit to Masjid Tanah — police/authority notification if needed | Logistics Coordinator |
| 7.6 | Crane offload at new yard and place into pre-marked heavy zone | Rigging Supervisor |
| 7.7 | Update inventory — confirm each heavy item landed correctly | Inventory Lead |

**Exit Gate:** All Class B items placed in designated zones at new yard

---

## Stage 8 — Office Equipment & Filing Cabinets (Week 5–6)

**Objective:** Move remaining IT equipment, filing cabinets, and office-related items.

| # | Task | Owner |
|---|------|-------|
| 8.1 | Pack remaining IT equipment and cabinets | IT Lead |
| 8.2 | Load and transport to new yard | Logistics Coordinator |
| 8.3 | Install IT equipment and set up basic connectivity at new yard | IT Lead |
| 8.4 | Place filing cabinets in designated document storage area | Warehouse Lead |
| 8.5 | Archive or dispose of non-essential documents per sort (Stage 5.5) | Warehouse Lead |

**Exit Gate:** IT and document storage functional at new yard

---

## Stage 9 — Loose Items & Consumables Move (Weeks 6–7)

**Objective:** Move the long tail — small loose items, consumables, hand tools, general spares.

| # | Task | Owner |
|---|------|-------|
| 9.1 | Pack consumables and loose items into labelled bins/crates | Warehouse Lead |
| 9.2 | Stage items at old yard loading point in dispatch order | Warehouse Lead |
| 9.3 | Multiple vehicle runs as needed (smaller loads) | Logistics Coordinator |
| 9.4 | Place into shelving/racking at new yard by category | Warehouse Lead |
| 9.5 | Final inventory reconciliation — all items accounted for | Inventory Lead |

**Exit Gate:** 100% inventory reconciliation passed · All items in assigned zones

---

## Stage 10 — Settle, Commission & Close-Out (Week 8)

**Objective:** Finalise everything at the new yard, close out the old yard, and formally end the project.

| # | Task | Owner |
|---|------|-------|
| 10.1 | Full audit of new yard against inventory — 100% match required | Inventory Lead |
| 10.2 | Commission any new yard infrastructure (CCTV, lighting, alarms) | Security Lead |
| 10.3 | Final clean-up of old yard | Operations Lead |
| 10.4 | Hand back old yard to landlord / confirm lease termination | PMO Lead |
| 10.5 | Close all authority licences and permits | PMO Lead |
| 10.6 | Final project review meeting — lessons learned | PMO Lead |
| 10.7 | Project sign-off and archive all documents | PMO Lead |

**Exit Gate:** Project formally closed · All stakeholders signed off · Lessons learned documented
