## Background

The existing ROV yard in Pasir Gudang has experienced **repeated burglaries**, resulting in theft of hand tools, metal stock, and copper cabling. Inventory is disorganised across three storage zones (warehouse, roofed yard, open yard), with large heavy items, small loose items, and consumables mixed without systematic segregation. Office furniture at the site is earmarked for sale or disposal. The decision has been made to **relocate the entire yard to Masjid Tanah**, complete with a new yard layout designed for safekeeping of engineering equipment and materials.

---

## Project Objectives

1. **Zero loss / zero theft** during the relocation — every item accounted for.
2. **Complete relocation within 8 weeks** (two months or earlier).
3. **Categorised, organised storage** at the new yard upon arrival.
4. **Full compliance** with local authorities at both Pasir Gudang (move-out) and Masjid Tanah (move-in).
5. **Sale or disposal** of all office furniture before final handover of the old yard.
