## 5. Reconciliation of Earlier Figures

Three figures circulated before this rerun. All were internally inconsistent.

| Figure | Where it appeared | Value (RM) | Why it was wrong |
|---|---|---:|---|
| Draft summary total | Superseded executive-summary table | 99,290 | Used a different, earlier set of category subtotals (A = 8,400 vs the detailed 21,700). Removed. |
| Original stated total | This report, §3 | 123,165 | The line items sum to **124,065** — RM 900 higher. |
| Revised stated total | `Blue_vs_Red_Costing_Discussion.md` | 103,265 | Its own revised column sums to **104,165** — RM 900 higher. |

### Root causes found

1. **RM 900 arithmetic gap** in both stated totals — the stated figure sat below the sum of its own components.
2. **Category B double-counted the inventory removal.** Resolution 1 says *"Budget inventory team once under Category B (RM 10,000). Remove duplicate M1 line"* — B keeps the RM 10,000, only M1 is removed (net −RM 10,000). The review's summary table instead reduced B from 11,800 to 1,800 **and** M from 24,800 to 14,800 — a double removal of RM 20,000.
3. **Contingency base was wrong.** N1 was labelled "10% of subtotals A–M" but RM 9,065 is 10% of RM 90,650, not of the actual A–M base.

### Result of the rerun

| | RM |
|---|---:|
| Original line items, as first written | 124,065 |
| N1 mis-stated — RM 9,065 written vs RM 11,245 correct (10% of A–M) | +2,180 |
| **Original estimate — corrected** | **126,245** |
| Net adjustments applied | −10,360 |
| **Revised estimate** | **115,885** |

> If the 10% contingency is held at the original RM 9,065 rather than recomputed, the total is **RM 114,665**. This report recomputes it, because the line's own definition requires it.

## 7. KEY ASSUMPTIONS & RISK NOTES

1. **Move duration:** 8 weeks (6–7 achievable with acceleration). Weather or permit delays may extend.
2. **Vendor quotations:** Estimates based on prevailing market rates; 3 quotes per vendor required before award.
3. **Inventory completeness:** 100% inventory by Week 2 is critical path — any delay cascades to all move stages.
4. **IMCA confirmation:** Must be confirmed before Week 1 ends — physical moves of training equipment cannot commence until cleared.
5. **New yard readiness:** Gate 3 must be passed before any shipment — secure cage, CCTV and utilities operational.
6. **Currency & validity:** All figures in RM; valid for 30 days from report date.
7. **Revenue offset:** Scrap metal and furniture sale proceeds credited back to the project budget (not included in the total).
8. **Contingency basis:** N1 is 10% of the revised A–M base. N2 and N3 are specific provisions retained on top, per the review's Resolution 2.
