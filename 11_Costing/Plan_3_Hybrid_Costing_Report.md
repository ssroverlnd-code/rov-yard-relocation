## 1. Executive Summary

This report provides a comprehensive tabulation of all resources required, estimated expenditures, and financial incursions anticipated for the execution of Plan 3 (Hybrid) — the recommended approach for relocating the ROV Yard from Pasir Gudang to Masjid Tanah. The plan combines formal governance (Blue Team) with operational execution detail (Red Team) across 8 stages with 7 management gates.

## 5. KEY ASSUMPTIONS & RISK NOTES

1. **Move duration:** Estimated at 8 weeks (6–7 achievable with acceleration). Weather or permit delays may extend.
2. **Vendor quotations:** All estimates based on prevailing market rates; 3 quotes per vendor required before award.
3. **Inventory completeness:** 100% inventory by Week 2 is critical path — any delay cascades to all move stages.
4. **IMCA confirmation:** Must be confirmed before Week 1 ends — physical moves of training equipment cannot commence until cleared.
5. **New yard readiness:** Gate 3 must be passed before any shipment — secure cage, CCTV, and utilities operational.
6. **Currency & validity:** All estimates in RM; figures valid for 30 days from report date.
7. **Revenue offset:** Scrap metal and furniture sale proceeds will be credited back to project budget (not included in total).
