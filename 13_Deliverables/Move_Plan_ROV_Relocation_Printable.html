1. Executive Summary

This document outlines the plan for relocating the ROV (Remotely Operated Vehicle) unit and associated assets from the current Kota Masai Yard to the Pengkalan Balak Yard in Masjid Tanah, Melaka. The relocation supports the newly established "Under Water Services" (UWS) Business Unit under the Melaka Branch, in collaboration with Turcomp Engineering Services Sdn Bhd.

Work is delegated across the SS Rover internal team by workstream — governance and approval (Managing Director), operations and HSE (Operation Manager), procurement and contracts (Procurement Manager), and execution (Relocation Lead, Warehouse Executive, Supervisor) — with Turcomp as receiving-party focal point.

2. Objectives

  • Safely relocate all ROV assets from Kota Masai to Pengkalan Balak Yard
  • Zero loss or damage to equipment during transfer
  • Minimal operational downtime during transition
  • Clear inventory handover with full asset verification
  • Compliance with both SS Rover and Turcomp HSE requirements
  • Clear delegation of authority — every task has one accountable owner

5. Relocation Phases

Sorting & tagging standard (5S): items are sorted first, then packed and tagged at package level — nothing moves without a package tag ([ZONE]-[CLASS]-PKG-[SEQ]) and a contents list fixed to the package. Sequence: Sort → Set in order → Shine → Standardise → Sustain. Tagging loose items before the wanted / unwanted decision wastes tags on items that are then disposed of.
