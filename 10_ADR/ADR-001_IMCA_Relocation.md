# ADR-001: IMCA Implications — ROV Training Establishment Relocation

**Status:** OPEN — Pending confirmation  
**Raised:** W1 (19 October 2026)  
**Raised by:** Project Team  
**Priority:** CRITICAL — Blocks physical moves of training-related equipment  
**Owner:** Training Management + Project Manager

---

## 1. Background

The existing ROV yard in Pasir Gudang supports an **IMCA-approved ROV training establishment**. The relocation to Masjid Tanah involves moving training-related equipment, spares, and possibly training infrastructure. The initial plan explicitly flags this as a scope boundary:

> *"Regulatory and training approval implications of moving an IMCA-approved ROV training establishment must be checked separately before commencing affected activities at the new site."*

---

## 2. Issue Statement

**What happens to IMCA approval when the training establishment relocates from Pasir Gudang to Masjid Tanah?**

Specific questions:
1. Does the current IMCA approval transfer automatically to the new location, or is a new application required?
2. Is a site inspection / reassessment required by IMCA or the awarding body at Masjid Tanah?
3. Are there facility, equipment, or documentation requirements that the new yard must meet before training can commence?
4. What is the lead time for any IMCA re-approval process?
5. Can training activities continue at Pasir Gudang during the transition, or must they pause?
6. Are there contractual or insurance implications if training is interrupted?

---

## 3. Affected Activities

| Activity | Impact if IMCA Not Confirmed |
|----------|------------------------------|
| Moving ROV training equipment | Cannot dispatch — training asset integrity at risk |
| Moving ROV spares and simulators | Same as above |
| Setting up new yard training areas | Cannot commission without approval |
| Scheduling training courses | Cannot commit to dates until location is approved |
| Insurance coverage | Policy may not cover unapproved training locations |

---

## 4. Options

| Option | Description | Pros | Cons |
|--------|-------------|------|------|
| **A — Confirm before move** | Verify IMCA requirements before any training-related equipment moves | Zero regulatory risk | May delay W4 moves by 1–2 weeks |
| **B — Move non-training items first** | Relocate non-training items (heavy equipment, consumables, furniture) while IMCA is confirmed in parallel | Keeps project moving | Training equipment stays at old yard longer |
| **C — Assume transfer** | Assume IMCA approval transfers and move everything on schedule | No delay | High risk if IMCA disagrees — potential training suspension |

---

## 5. Recommendation

**Option A (preferred) with Option B (parallel):**

1. **Week 1:** Training Management contacts IMCA / awarding body to confirm requirements and lead time.
2. **Weeks 1–4:** Proceed with all non-training moves (heavy equipment, consumables, furniture, IT) per Plan 3 schedule.
3. **Hold point:** Training-related equipment dispatch is blocked until IMCA confirmation is received.
4. **If IMCA requires >4 weeks:** Escalate to Management — may need to adjust project scope or timeline.
