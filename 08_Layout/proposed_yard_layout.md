# Proposed New Yard Layout — Masjid Tanah

## Design Principles

1. **Security first** — high-value items in a locked, monitored cage closest to the office.
2. **Heavy on hard standing** — Class B items placed on reinforced concrete, away from pedestrian traffic.
3. **Consumables accessible** — shelving near the loading point for fast dispatch.
4. **Vehicle flow** — one-way loop so heavy vehicles don't reverse within the yard.
5. **Fire safety** — combustible consumables stored away from ignition sources; fire extinguishers at each zone.
6. **Future expansion** — zones sized 20% above current inventory to allow growth.

---

## Yard Zones

### Zone 1 — Secure Cage (Class A: High Value)

| Attribute | Detail |
|-----------|--------|
| Location | Adjacent to office, most visible from CCTV |
| Construction | 2.4 m chain-link cage with lockable gate, roofed |
| Contents | Copper cables, hand tools, precision instruments, critical spares |
| Access | Security Lead only; dual-key control (Security Lead + PMO Lead) |
| Monitoring | Dedicated CCTV camera, motion sensor alarm |
| Inventory | Item-level manifest posted inside cage; updated after every access |

### Zone 2 — Heavy Items Hard Standing (Class B)

| Attribute | Detail |
|-----------|--------|
| Location | Central yard on reinforced concrete pad |
| Marking | Yellow painted border lines, item positions pre-marked |
| Contents | ROV components, structural steel, skids, large containers |
| Access | Rigging Supervisor controls placement; crane lift directly into position |
| Load rating | Minimum 5 tonnes/m² — confirmed by Operations Lead before first lift |

### Zone 3 — Shelving & Racking (Class C: Consumables)

| Attribute | Detail |
|-----------|--------|
| Location | North side of yard, near loading bay |
| Construction | Heavy-duty steel racking, 4 levels, labelled shelves |
| Contents | Fasteners, seals, lubricants, hand tools (non-Class A), general spares |
| Labelling | Shelf labels: category, sub-category, date stocked |
| Access | Warehouse Lead issues; stock issued against requisition |

### Zone 4 — Document & Filing Storage

| Attribute | Detail |
|-----------|--------|
| Location | Inside office or lockable container |
| Contents | Filing cabinets (essential records), archive boxes |
| Access | Warehouse Lead; archive access by authorisation |
| Fire safety | Fire-resistant cabinet for critical documents |

### Zone 5 — Loading & Vehicle Access

| Attribute | Detail |
|-----------|--------|
| Location | Yard entrance, widest point |
| Marking | White lines marking vehicle path and loading zone |
| Vehicle flow | One-way loop: enter → load/unload → exit |
| Parking | Designated parking for staff vehicles outside loading zone |

---

## Security Infrastructure

| Measure | Location | Responsible |
|---------|----------|-------------|
| Perimeter fencing (2.4 m) | Full yard boundary | Operations Lead |
| CCTV (4+ cameras) | Entrance, Zone 1, Zone 2, Zone 5 | Security Lead |
| Motion-sensor floodlights | All zones, activated at dusk | Operations Lead |
| Alarm system | Zone 1 + office | Security Lead |
| Secure cage lock | Zone 1 | Security Lead |
| Fire extinguishers | Each zone (2 per zone) | Warehouse Lead |
| Sign-in / sign-out board | Entrance | Warehouse Lead |

## Pre-Move Requirements

Before any item arrives at the new yard, confirm:

- [ ] Perimeter fence intact and secure
- [ ] CCTV operational and recording
- [ ] Zone 1 secure cage locked, alarm tested
- [ ] Zone 2 heavy standing marked and load-rated
- [ ] Zone 3 shelving installed and labelled
- [ ] Fire extinguishers placed in all zones
- [ ] Power, water, and welfare facilities operational
- [ ] Zone 5 vehicle route clear and marked
